Every page, every role, walked and scored.

Playwright drove both Sero apps as a logged-out visitor, a member, a manager and an internal admin. Every route loaded, every button listed, the safe ones tapped, every screen measured. Nothing paid, nothing written to live.

The verdict

Scorecard

Scored on what the walk showed, not on intent. Sero is in good shape. Nothing crashed, and every role wall held.

  • 6.5/10UX

    Short flows, mostly-right structure. Held back by the refresh dead end, the Team and Members muddle, and how thin the member side is.

  • 6/10UI

    Handsome and consistent. One shell, one card style, one grid. Marked down for the button contrast and the sidebar stopping mid page.

  • 7/10Writing

    The screen copy is the best thing in the product. Marked down because the em-dash rule is broken in 35 of 41 briefings.

  • 4.5/10Motion

    Effectively absent. Hover states only, no transitions, no visible loading.

  • 7/10Sense

    It hangs together. The gaps are at the seams: two people lists, an admin shell that ejects managers, one screen that is only a card.

18 findings

What the walk found

2 critical, 5 high, 6 medium, 5 low. Tap any one to open the evidence. Every screenshot is the real rendered screen.

criticalUX·Manager F1Open a past 1:1, press refresh, lose the page

Tap a person in Past 1:1s and the address becomes /runs/<id>. Reload that exact address and Sero rewrites it back to /runs and shows an empty card: “NO 1:1 SELECTED. Pick one from your 1:1s list.” Browser Back is dead too. The address returns, the empty card stays.

It is Sero's own URL. Refresh, bookmark, or open it on another device and you hit a dead end, with nothing on screen saying the 1:1 still exists.

Fix Boot needs to honour /runs/:id the way it already honours /team/:person. The router parses the id fine; boot never acts on it outside the member lane.

criticalUI·Everyone F2Every primary button fails colour contrast

The action blue #5aa9e6 with white text measures 2.54:1 against a 4.5:1 bar. It is on Log in, Start 1:1, Add person, Invite people, Continue, Add request and Save note. The whole action layer, both apps, all three roles.

The most important control on every screen is the hardest thing on it to read. In daylight the label sinks into the fill and it reads as disabled. Your own DESIGN.md rule 2 asks for 4.5:1.

Fix The palette already holds the answer. --sero-primary-800 (#1b5d91) gives 6.97:1. Keep #5aa9e6 for accents, active nav and links. Change only the solid-button fill. One token, whole app.

highWriting·Every manager who reads a briefing F7The em-dash rule is broken inside the briefings

npm run lint:copy passes, and it is not wrong. It reads admin/src and frontend/src, 248 files, and those are clean. Everything downstream is unguarded.

  • 35 of 41 finished briefings in the dev org contain em dashes
  • The Meeting arcs screen renders 7
  • 144 quoted em-dash strings live in backend/engine outside the tests
  • answer-suggester.ts:56 tells the model: Use “→” for cause/result and “—” for a trailing detail when it fits.

The rule exists because these are the words a manager reads before a real conversation. The engine is being taught to break it, so the more Sero writes, the worse it gets.

Fix Three steps. Delete the em-dash instruction in answer-suggester.ts:56. Widen SCAN_DIRS to backend/engine and content/data, then clear what it finds. Then guard the generated prose where it is saved, because a source scan can never catch what the model writes at runtime.

highPermissions·Manager F16A manager can demote an admin

On Members, the row menu on an admin row offers Make manager and Switch off access to a plain manager. Behind it, setRole accepts any target in the org and only guards against removing the last active lead. Nothing checks that the person doing it outranks the person it is done to. Deactivation is better protected: a superadmin email cannot be switched off.

Anyone you invite as a manager can quietly change the role of the account that runs the workspace. A trust question rather than a bug, but the door is open.

Fix Add a rank check to setRole: a manager may change a member or another manager, never an admin. Then hide the menu on admin rows for non-admins.

Read from the code path and the rendered menu. The mutation itself was not fired.

highUI·Admin F3The sidebar stops halfway down every long page

The app shell is sized to the window, not the document. It renders 900px tall on Library (2,641px of page), User management (2,115px), All runs (11,680px) and Design system (12,040px). Below 900px the navy nav simply stops and a bare white column runs to the bottom.

Scroll down the Library and the app looks broken. The shell falls away and the content floats on white.

Fix Sticky nav at height: 100vh inside a full-height flex shell, or paint the shell background on the layout container so the column reads as deliberate.

highUI·Manager, Admin F4Header buttons collide with the account chip

Measured at 1440×900: the account chip sits at x 1108 to 1428, y 12 to 52. On Past 1:1s the Start 1:1 button sits at y 48 to 96, and on Library Archived (7) sits at y 48 to 96. Both tuck under the chip. Pages carrying the small eyebrow line (“Work”, “Account”) push the button clear. Pages without one do not.

Two of the most-used buttons in the product read as clipped, and the header stops looking designed.

Fix Have the eyebrow slot reserve its height when empty, and give the header a min-height so the action always clears the chip.

highUX·Manager F5“Team” and “Members” sit side by side and neither name explains itself

Team is the people you run 1:1s with. Members is the accounts that can log in. They are adjacent in the nav, both are lists of people with avatars, and the same human can appear in both.

This is the one place a new manager holds two meanings of “person” at once. Adding someone to the wrong list gets them nothing, with no error to explain it.

Fix Name them by intent: “My people” and “Workspace access”. Or fold access into Team as a column and drop the second screen.

mediumUX·Admin F8The error log shows raw SQL to a human

103 issues, 10,047px of page, no paging, and the “What went wrong” column prints whole insert statements. 3.3 million characters of text on one screen.

It is the screen you open when something is on fire, and it is the least readable screen in the product.

Fix Show the first clause only, keep the full statement in the row detail, page at 50.

mediumUI·Everyone F17The build stamp blocks clicks, and it ships to live

The build chip is a fixed button, 243×27px, bottom right, z-index 9999, pointer-events auto, on every screen of both apps. elementFromPoint at its centre returns the chip, so nothing under it can be clicked. Its own source calls it “an always-on chip”, not a dev-only one. It swallowed roughly 30 clicks during the automated pass.

It sits where a last table row, a pagination control or a footer action lands. Invisible as a problem until someone cannot click something and has no idea why.

Fix pointer-events: none on the chip, with auto only on the SHA span if click-to-copy matters.

mediumUX·Member, Manager F18“Add request” with an empty box does nothing and says nothing

On the member's Home, tapping Add request with the box empty produces no change at all. No error, no hint, and the button is not disabled. Confirmed by comparing the full page text before and after. On intake the same situation is handled properly: Continue with nothing chosen says “Pick someone, or add a name to continue.”

It is the member's only interactive control in the entire product. Silence teaches them it is broken.

Fix Reuse the intake pattern: one line under the field. The copy already exists in that voice.

mediumUX·Member F10The member's side of Sero is one card deep

A member can reach exactly one screen. Every other address, including their own 1:1, bounces to /home. Home shows a single latest 1:1 (date and type only), a Requests box, and an empty Goals card. The nav says “Your 1:1s”; the page says “Home” and lists one.

Half the value story is that the member sees something too. Today that is a date, a label and a text box.

Fix Own it or open it. Match the nav label to the page and say plainly why only the date is shown, or give the list its own screen. The no-inference ruling limits what is shown, not how well it is framed.

mediumUI·Admin F6Six brand marks are broken on the Design system page

The Brandmark row asks for /sero-flowbite/brand/*.svg and gets six 404s. The files exist. They sit behind the admin app's /admin base, so the root-absolute path misses them.

The page meant to be the source of truth for the brand renders the brand as missing images.

Fix Prefix with import.meta.env.BASE_URL, the way google-g.svg already does.

mediumUI·Everyone F9Click targets under the comfortable minimum

Row overflow menus are 34×26px, the nav collapse is 28×28px, star ratings 25×24px, the account chip 28×22px, filter checkboxes 18×18px. The floor is 44×44 for touch and 32×32 at the very least for a mouse.

These are the controls reached for most: rename a person, open a run, rate a prep.

Fix Grow the hit area without changing the look. A ::before inset of -8px, or a 44px min tap box.

lowUX·Manager, Member F13Half a screen of empty space on most pages

Measured at 1440×900: the manager's Home ends at 460px of 900, the member's Home at 589px, Past 1:1s at 614px, Team at 654px, Members at 686px. On intake the column runs 556 to 1132px, leaving a 308px dead gutter and an off-centre look.

The product looks like it holds less than it does. There is room for the next 1:1 due, the person's recent themes, or the prep left half finished.

Fix The guest lane already gets this right. Do the same signed in, then give the list pages a right rail.

lowMotion·Everyone F12Nothing moves

Across 256 page loads, 3 pages carried a running CSS animation, and the average page had 33 elements with a transition, almost all hover colour changes. No page transition, no list stagger, no animated state change, and no skeleton in the DOM on a settled page.

The app feels correct but inert. Motion is usually where a product says someone thought about this. Here it says nothing.

Fix Three cheap wins: a 120ms cross-fade on stage change, a 40ms-per-row stagger on list mount, and a skeleton that is actually visible on first fetch.

lowWriting·Manager, Admin F11Two date formats in the same list

Home and Past 1:1s mix relative and absolute in adjacent rows: “2d ago”, “5d ago”, then “Fri 17 Jul 2026”. User management goes further with “last active no runs yet · 0 runs”.

You cannot compare two rows without doing arithmetic, and “last active no runs yet” is not a sentence.

Fix Relative under seven days, absolute after, applied to the whole column rather than per row. Write the empty state as “never active”.

lowUI·Logged out F14The logged-out screens are not in the brand typeface

Every in-app screen sets headings in Bricolage Grotesque. The front door, Log in, Create account and the password screens set theirs in Inter. That is 74 of 256 page loads.

The three screens a new manager meets first are the three that do not look like Sero.

Fix Apply the display face in auth.css, or decide it is deliberate and write that down in DESIGN.md.

lowUI·Everyone F15Search boxes have no name for a screen reader

The list toolbar's search input carries a placeholder and nothing else. No label, no aria-label. It appears on 10 screens including Team, Members, Library and User management.

A screen-reader user hears “edit text” and guesses, and the placeholder vanishes the moment they type.

Fix One aria-label in list-toolbar.ts fixes all ten.

How this was run

Method

Driver
Playwright on Chrome 150, 1440×900, a clean browser context per role
Where
Local dev against real Postgres, build 4e746ca9
Accounts
Three purpose-made accounts in the Sero (dev) org, one per role, the member linked to a roster person
Data
Five finished 1:1s cloned from existing dev runs, plus a five-person team. No OpenAI calls. Cost: nothing.
Three passes
Load every route as every role and screenshot it. Tap every safe button and record what happened. Then measure contrast, target size, naming and heading order per element.
Not covered
Phone widths, the guest run end to end (it starts a paid engine run), and anything behind a destructive button
Two things that looked like bugs and were not Duplicate people on the Team page, and a briefing about “Maya” on Nina Petrova's card, were both artefacts of the cloned seed data rather than the app. Both were checked against the database and the API before being dropped.
