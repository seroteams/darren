One thing worth saying out loud You chose the merge over the rename for Team and Members. It is the only item on the list that is a build rather than a fix, and it moves the invite flow, which is why it sits last in the plan. Everything else lands and gets tested before the structure changes underneath it. If the mock above does not look like a win over just renaming the two screens, that is the moment to say so.
