**Result: 5 questions, 5 with their own coaching. The prep-brief fallback did not appear once.**

## Question 1 — hand-written opener (Phase 3)
**What's been on your mind since we last spoke?**

- **How to ask** Ask it warm and open. It is a door, not a status request.
- **Listen for** Whether they pick up a thread from last time or start fresh.
- **Listen for** What they choose to lead with.

## Question 2 — invented mid-meeting by the planner (Phase 2)
**What have you tried so far with the QA environment, and what happened?**

- **How to ask** Keep it on what he tried, not a full postmortem.
- **Listen for** Whether he names a concrete fix attempt or stays at problem level.
- **Listen for** What result each attempt had on beta or live-date planning.

## Question 3 — bank question, reworded mid-meeting (Phase 1 + 2)
**What actually caused the last two sprint slips?**

- **How to ask** Keep it to the last two slips, not the whole quarter.
- **Listen for** Whether he names one cause per slip or stays broad.
- **Listen for** Whether QA, handover, or something else shows up first.

## Question 4 — bank question, reworded mid-meeting (Phase 1 + 2)
**What are you choosing to protect in beta, and what are you willing to drop?**

- **How to ask** Ask for one thing he will protect and one he would cut.
- **Listen for** Whether he names quality, scope, or date first.
- **Listen for** Whether the trade-off makes the live date clearer.

## Question 5 — bank question, reworded mid-meeting (Phase 1 + 2)
**What would make the next two weeks smoother for you?**

- **How to ask** Keep it open: smoother can mean support, decisions, or fewer distractions.
- **Listen for** Whether he asks for a decision, a trade-off, or cover with another team.
- **Listen for** Whether he names one concrete change for the next two weeks.
