The one that failed

A person's page squashes their name into a thin column

Customer app · Team · click any person who has had a 1:1

The name, job title and the "2 1:1s · last 23d ago · 4.5 prep rating" line get crushed into a column about 190px wide and wrap over three lines, while half the header sits empty. On the example person it is worse: 60px, and the name breaks mid-air.

It is not the example chip's fault on its own. The header row is capped at 576px and the blue button ("Start 1:1 with qa-overnight Priya") takes 285px of it, so the identity block gets whatever is left. The chip then takes another 81px.

This is new information for two plans: demo-member Phase 2 put the chip there, and the audit's small sweep fixed the dangling middot on this exact line. The middot fix does work (I measured it: the separator always wraps with its item). The header around it is the problem.

.page-header__row 576px .page-header__actions 285px ← the blue button .rd-profile__id 191px ← name + role + meta, all of it with the Example chip: 60px

Customer app · during a 1:1 · Live scores

Members is a screen with no door

Customer app · nav rail

The rail offers Home, Start 1:1, Team, Past 1:1s. There is no Members link, yet /members is a full working screen and it is where the permissions fix lives. The audit plan's own "done means" says Team and Members should be one screen called My Team. That part has not been built.

The Managers table on Pulse hides its last column

Admin app · Pulse

The table is 690px inside a 627px card, so the Status column is cut off. It scrolls sideways inside the card, so nothing is lost, but at normal width you cannot see it.
